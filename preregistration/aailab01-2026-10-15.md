## Decision rules (fixed before results)

- fetch-live: all seven checks pass. A clean-fetch retry seen is recorded,
  not a failure.
- corpus / network / openmm / suite: every failure is read and classified as
  in CONTRIBUTING.md (expected_limitation / validation_failed / failed /
  environment). 6B73 must get past setup. A `validation_failed` is a defect.
- smoke: statuses tabulated; any `validation_failed` is a defect; `failed`
  with a library traceback is a defect.
- One CUDA process at a time. No output directory is reused or overwritten;
  a rerun gets a new run-NNN.

## run-008 / run-009 (2026-10-16): cost against size

gpu_shakedown.py, CUDA mixed, --ns 1 --segments 2. Both segments resumed in each, and each join cost 0 frames of volume settling (1AO6 at 1040 nm^3, 2GLS at 3238 nm^3).

| Run | Structure | Particles | k (s per particle-step) | Fit spread | ns/day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| run-004 | 1UBQ | 13,247 | 2.525e-08 | 1.10 | ~515 |
| run-005 | 1CAG | 64,616 | 1.623e-08 | 1.03 | |
| run-008 | 1AO6 | 103,988 | 1.549e-08 | 1.07 | ~108 |
| run-009 | 2GLS | 330,217 | 1.483e-08 | 1.01 | ~36 |

**Outcome.** Cost per particle is close to constant on this card from about 65k particles up: k varies 1.10x from 65k to 330k, and a power law fitted to those three has alpha 0.95 (worst error 1%). Only the small system is dearer per particle: k at 13k is 1.70x k at 330k, because a 13k system leaves the 4090 under-used. A single power law through all four (alpha 0.83) misses by up to 10%, so the shape is a floor, not a curve. For the cost model: a constant fitted at production size (60k and up) holds to about 10% across production sizes. Fitted on a small system, it overestimates a large one by up to 1.7x. Argon alone underestimates by 1.6–2.8x, the most for the smallest system.

No product change follows from this run alone. The fit already refuses a spread past 3, and every fit here was under 1.1.
